## Tunisian Agricultural Market Data

### MAJOR CROPS AND MARKETS

#### DURUM WHEAT (قمح ديور)
**Market Structure:**
- Domestic production: ~1.5 million tons/year
- Consumption: ~1.8 million tons/year (deficit of 300,000 tons)
- Imports: Mainly from France, Canada, Ukraine

**2023 Price Analysis:**
- Producer price: 400-500 TND/ton
- Milling price: 500-550 TND/ton
- Flour price: 800-900 TND/ton
- Pasta price: 3,000-4,000 TND/ton

**Government Support:**
- Guaranteed minimum price: 450 TND/ton
- Subsidy program for small farmers
- Export restrictions (for food security)

**Seasonal Pattern:**
- Harvest: June-July
- Price low: July-August (peak supply)
- Price high: April-May (pre-harvest)
- Spread: 100-150 TND/ton (35% variation)

**Market Opportunities:**
- Direct sales to mills (better price)
- Cooperative marketing
- Quality premiums for specific varieties
- Storage until price recovery

#### OLIVE OIL (زيت الزيتون)
**Market Structure:**
- Production: 250,000-400,000 tons/year (varies by season)
- Exports: 150,000-250,000 tons/year (60% of production)
- Major export destinations: EU (esp. Italy), Gulf states, USA

**2023 Price Analysis:**
- Producer price (bulk): 7-12 TND/liter
- Bottled price (retail): 15-25 TND/liter
- Premium brands: 30-50+ TND/liter
- Export FOB: 6-10 USD/liter

**Export Markets:**
- EU: 40% (bulk to Italy for blending)
- Gulf: 25% (premium bottled)
- Other: 35% (Americas, Asia, North Africa)

**Seasonal Pattern:**
- Harvest: Oct-Feb (peak production)
- Price low: Nov-Dec (harvest glut)
- Price high: May-Sept (low supply)
- Spread: 5-8 TND/liter (70% variation) - HIGHEST among commodities

**Market Opportunities:**
- Delay sales to high-price season (storage)
- Value-addition (bottling, organic certification)
- EU organic premium: 20-30% higher price
- Direct export agreements with Arab markets

**Quality Premiums:**
- Extra virgin (acidity <0.8%): +5 TND/liter
- Organic certified: +4-6 TND/liter
- Early harvest (fresh taste): +3-4 TND/liter

#### DATES (التمر)
**Market Structure:**
- Production: ~300,000 tons/year
- Exports: ~100,000 tons/year
- Major varieties: Deglet Noor (60%), Khalili, others

**2023 Price Analysis:**

**Deglet Noor (درع النور):**
- Producer price: 8-15 TND/kg (varies by quality)
- Export price: 10-20 USD/kg (for premium)
- Retail: 15-30 TND/kg

**Khalili (خليلي) and others:**
- Producer price: 3-6 TND/kg
- Lower export value
- Local market dominant

**Export Markets:**
- EU (France, Spain): 40% (high-quality)
- Gulf states: 35% (premium fresh)
- Other: 25% (North Africa, Asia)

**Seasonal Pattern:**
- Harvest: Sept-Oct
- Price high: Pre-harvest (July-Sept)
- Price low: Post-harvest (Oct-Dec)
- Spread: 8-12 TND/kg (100% variation)

**Market Opportunities:**
- Grade and sort for premium pricing
- Ramadan peak demand (30-40% price increase)
- Export to Gulf during Ramadan
- Value-add: Processed, packaged products

#### CITRUS (الحمضيات)
**Production:** ~350,000 tons/year (oranges, lemons, mandarins)

**2023 Price Analysis:**

**Orange:**
- Domestic: 400-600 TND/ton
- Export: 500-800 TND/ton

**Lemon:**
- Domestic: 500-800 TND/ton
- Export: 700-1,200 TND/ton
- Seasonal variation: 300-500 TND/ton (winter premium)

**Mandarin:**
- Premium: 1,000-1,500 TND/ton
- Early season (Dec-Jan): 30% premium

**Seasonal Pattern:**
- Winter harvest (Dec-Feb): HIGH prices
- Summer: Minimal supply, very high prices
- Export season: Dec-May (EU market)

**Export Markets:**
- EU: 60% (seasonal table fruit)
- Libya, Algeria: 25% (processing)
- Other: 15%

**Market Opportunities:**
- Early varieties: 20-30% premium
- Export-quality sorting
- Processing juice: Lower grade fruit
- Cold storage for off-season sales

#### TOMATO (الطماطم)
**Market Structure:**
- Domestic consumption: ~150,000 tons/year
- Exports: Fresh (seasonal) and processing

**2023 Price Analysis:**

**Off-season (Summer, May-Sept):**
- Greenhouse: 800-1,200 TND/ton
- Field hothouses: 600-900 TND/ton
- Export ready: 1,000-1,500 TND/ton

**In-season (Winter, Oct-April):**
- Field: 300-500 TND/ton
- Processing price: 200-300 TND/ton

**Processing (concentrate):**
- 28% brix: 600-800 TND/ton
- 30% brix: 800-1,000 TND/ton

**Seasonal Pattern:**
- Highest: May-July (off-season)
- Lowest: October-November (harvest)
- Spread: 700-900 TND/ton (300% variation) - EXTREME

**Export Markets:**
- Libya: 40% (fresh, seasonal)
- EU: 20% (early season)
- Processing: Algeria, Libya

**Market Opportunities:**
- Greenhouse off-season farming (extreme prices)
- Early season premium (30-50%)
- Processing contracts (stable price)
- Export-quality production

### RISK FACTORS IN TUNISIAN AGRICULTURE

**Climate Risks:**
- Drought: Increases prices but reduces volumes
- Frost (North): Citrus, olive damage (supply shock)
- Rainfall: Highly variable (50-1,000mm range)

**Market Risks:**
- Subsidy volatility: Government policy changes
- Exchange rate: Dinar depreciation increases export prices
- Import competition: Libya reopening could increase competition
- Price swings: Extreme volatility in vegetables

**Political/Economic Risks:**
- Water availability: Saharan aquifer depletion
- Labor costs: Increasing (inflation 8%+)
- Input costs: Fertilizer, fuel price volatility
- Export barriers: EU quality standards

### HEDGING AND PRICE PROTECTION STRATEGIES

**Forward Contracts:**
- Olive oil: 2-3 month forward common
- Wheat: Government guaranteed price option
- Legumes: Long-term contracts available

**Storage Strategy:**
- Cold storage for potatoes, citrus
- Olive oil cans/bottles for delayed sales
- Wheat silos for price timing
- Cost: 50-200 TND/ton/month

**Crop Insurance:**
- Limited availability in Tunisia
- Government subsidy programs
- Cooperative group insurance
- Cost: 5-10% of expected value

**Market Timing:**
- Delay sales 1-2 months: 15-30% price gain (common in vegetables)
- Early season premium: 20-50% (off-season crops)
- Quality improvement: 10-20% premium
- Direct export: 10-30% premium over middleman
