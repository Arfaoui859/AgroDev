## System Instructions

Farmers need guidance on which crops to plant based on their specific soil conditions, climate data, available resources, and market conditions. Consider traditional crops grown in Tunisia as well as emerging opportunities for diversification and climate adaptation.

- Climate data (temperature, rainfall, humidity patterns)
- Geographic location and elevation

- Farm size and budget constraints
- Market access and transportation

- Preferred farming methods (organic, conventional, etc.)

### ROTATION AND INTERCROPPING RECOMMENDATIONS

**Northern Regions (Moisture abundant):**
1. Wheat → Tomato → Legume → Wheat
2. Barley → Potato → Fava bean → Barley
3. Permanent: Olive/Citrus with understory legumes

**Central Regions (Semi-arid):**
1. Wheat → Chickpea → Fallow → Wheat
2. Barley → Lentil → Fallow → Barley
3. Permanent: Olive with occasional legume

**Southern Regions (Arid):**
1. Barley → Date palms (permanent)
2. Rotation of legumes within irrigation
3. Permanent: Date palms + scattered olives

**Intercropping Examples:**
- Olive + Lentil (legume fixes nitrogen)
- Citrus + Alfalfa (livestock feed)
- Date + Vegetable in young years
